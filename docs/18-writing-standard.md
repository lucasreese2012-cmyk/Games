# 18 — Writing Standard

Every document in this world bible describes the physical environment. It does not describe what anyone could *do* there, and it does not explain the places with invented history.

## Rules

1. **Describe the ground, not its use in a game.** Not "this mountain would be great for gameplay" but "the mountain rises 1,100 m above the valley, with a two-lane road climbing through hardwood forest before emerging onto bare granite near the summit."
2. **Give the physical reason.** Every place has a geographic justification: rock, water, slope, soil, exposure, access.
3. **Physical evidence, never narrative.** "A collapsed steel truss lies in the creek beside a concrete bridge" — not why it collapsed, who built it, or what happened there.
4. **Numbers come from the map.** Elevations, lengths, areas, depths, grades, drive times and sightlines are measured on the generated terrain and network, not estimated.
5. **No game-design vocabulary.** The banned list below is checked automatically on every build.
