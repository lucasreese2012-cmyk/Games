# 19 — Inspiration Matrix

For each island: the real-world regions that inform its geography, architecture and vegetation, and the games whose *principles* (not places) inform its atmosphere, exploration, landmarks and environmental storytelling — with the reason each was chosen. No specific location from any source is copied; each island's geometry is generated from its own geological logic.

**How the references were chosen.** Real-world regions supply what makes the place *believable*: the physical processes and the built forms that go with them. Games supply what makes it *memorable*: a particular way of framing a view, pacing discovery, or letting objects speak. Where a game is listed, the principle is the one described in [00 — Research](00-research-principles.md).
