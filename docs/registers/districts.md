## Halcomb Island

### Tennalee Falls Mill District

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** in Tennalee Falls (grid 36.9 E / 65.9 N)
- **Urban form:** mill district
- **Buildings:** 10–30 m tall, 60% of ground built or paved
- **Ground elevation:** 18 m
- **Extent:** about 0.8 km²
- **Description:** Brick textile mills four and five storeys high along the river below the falls, with a tall chimney and a mill race.
- **Geographic justification:** Water power at the fall line.
- **Visual identity:** Red brick mills beside white water.
- **Nearby features:** Tennalee Falls, Main Line station.
- **Why it is memorable:** The falls roaring between the mills.

## Calder Island

### Downtown Calder

- **Island:** Calder Island
- **Location:** in Calder (grid 37.8 E / 61.9 N)
- **Urban form:** high-rise core
- **Buildings:** 40–270 m tall, 75% of ground built or paved
- **Ground elevation:** 10 m
- **Extent:** about 1.8 km²
- **Description:** The central business district on the bluff at the river mouth: glass and limestone towers up to 64 storeys, the old customs house, and narrow streets laid out on the colonial grid.
- **Geographic justification:** The first dry ground above the deep anchorage at the Tennalee mouth; land values here have always been the highest on the island.
- **Visual identity:** A tight cluster of towers on a bluff, the tallest crowned with a lit spire.
- **Nearby features:** Old Market, Station Square, Market Street Bridge, Calder Tower.
- **Why it is memorable:** The skyline rising straight out of the water when seen from the Tennalee River Bridge.

- **Ground elevation:** 4 m
- **Extent:** about 0.6 km²

- **Visual identity:** Red brick, iron balconies and cobblestones.
- **Nearby features:** Calder Wharves, Downtown, Ferry Landing.
- **Why it is memorable:** Cobbles glistening after summer storms.
