## Halcomb Island

### Ledford Dome

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Centre of the Balsam Crest, 1.7 km east of Ledford Gap — 8.5 km SSE of Pigeonroost
- **Elevation:** 2,047 m (surveyed terrain 2,047 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,543 m within 3 km
- **Form:** dome summit
- **Description:** The highest point in the archipelago: a broad, rounded summit wrapped in dark red spruce and Fraser fir, crowned by a concrete observation tower reached by a spiral ramp. Grey trunks of dead fir stand among the living trees on the windward south-west shoulder.
- **Geographic justification:** The crest is the core of the old crystalline massif, where resistant gneiss has outlasted the surrounding schists; at 2,047 m it is high enough for a relict boreal forest even at 30° N.
- **Visual identity:** Black-green cone of conifers above a paler band of hardwoods, often capped in cloud while the valleys are clear; the tower's white ramp is visible from Calder on clear winter mornings.
- **Nearby features:** Ledford Gap, the Parkway, Ledford Dome Road parking lot, Coldspring Knob, the Pigeonroost and Ledford Prong headwaters.
- **Why it is memorable:** From the tower the whole archipelago lies below — the North Face dropping to the Narrows, Calloway Lake, the city on its island, and the flat southern islands fading into haze.

### Mount Sawyer

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Balsam Crest, 3.5 km east of Ledford Dome — 8.0 km SW of Big Laurel
- **Elevation:** 1,985 m (surveyed terrain 1,985 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,395 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A long, double-topped summit ridge under spruce–fir with grassy openings where old fir blowdowns never regrew. A rough trail follows the crest between its two tops.
- **Geographic justification:** Second culmination of the same resistant gneiss band that forms Ledford Dome; the crest is continuous here because no stream has cut back through it.
- **Visual identity:** A level skyline of dark conifers seen from the south as a wall above Calloway Lake.
- **Nearby features:** Mount Callahan spur, Hemlock Knob, Callahan Branch headwaters, the Parkway.
- **Why it is memorable:** Its flat top makes the crest look like a rampart running east from Ledford Dome.

### Mount Callahan

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** North Face, on the spur north of Mount Sawyer — 5.3 km SW of Big Laurel
- **Elevation:** 1,955 m (surveyed terrain 1,955 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,791 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A massive, free-standing mountain that rises almost 2,000 m directly from the Narrows in less than five kilometres. Grey cliffs of slate and phyllite break out on its north-west face below a summit of spruce, fir and heath.
- **Geographic justification:** A thrust slice of harder metasediment pushed north over the softer rocks; the Narrows strait has cut away its base, leaving the steepest relief in the islands.
- **Visual identity:** Seen from the mainland bridge it looks like a separate peak standing in front of the crest, with pale cliffs catching the late sun.
- **Nearby features:** Callahan Branch, Big Laurel Creek, the Narrows Road, Pigeonroost.
- **Why it is memorable:** The mountain fills the windshield the whole way across the Narrows Bridge.

### Coldspring Knob

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Balsam Crest, west of Ledford Gap — 13.1 km E of Coldwater
- **Elevation:** 1,905 m (surveyed terrain 1,905 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,414 m within 3 km
- **Form:** knob
- **Description:** A sharp knob with a cluster of steel relay towers and their equipment huts on a cleared summit, surrounded by northern hardwoods and spruce.
- **Geographic justification:** Part of the main crest; its summit was cleared because it has the clearest radio path to both Calder and the mainland.
- **Visual identity:** Red aircraft lights blink on its towers at night above Ledford.
- **Nearby features:** Ledford Gap, Thunderstone spur, Brushy Mountain, the Parkway.
- **Why it is memorable:** The towers are the first thing travellers see from the Gap Road switchbacks below.

### Hemlock Knob

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Balsam Crest, between Mount Sawyer and The Steeples — 6.2 km NW of Upper Cove
- **Elevation:** 1,872 m (surveyed terrain 1,872 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,371 m within 3 km
- **Form:** knob
- **Description:** A rounded summit whose north slope holds a stand of dead and dying hemlocks — grey, needle-less trunks above a dense rhododendron understory.
- **Geographic justification:** Cool, north-facing slope where hemlock once dominated the coves below the spruce zone.
- **Visual identity:** A pale grey patch on an otherwise dark green flank, visible from the North Face.
- **Nearby features:** Big Laurel Creek headwaters, Huckleberry Knob, the Parkway.
- **Why it is memorable:** The grey "ghost forest" on its shoulder is unlike any other slope on the island.

### Painted Bald

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Western Balsam Crest, above the Hominy headwaters — 10.3 km E of Coldwater
- **Elevation:** 1,745 m (surveyed terrain 1,745 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,232 m within 3 km
- **Form:** grassy bald
- **Description:** A treeless summit meadow of oatgrass and blueberry ringed by stunted beech, ablaze with flame azalea in June and red blueberry leaves in October.
- **Geographic justification:** Exposed summit swept by winter winds and ice, where an open meadow has persisted for generations.
- **Visual identity:** A tawny bald patch on the dark crest, unmistakable from Hominy Bay.
- **Nearby features:** Sugartree Knob, Stormhead Mountain, the Parkway overlooks.
- **Why it is memorable:** The only place on the crest where you can see in every direction while standing in grass.

### Stormhead Mountain

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Western Balsam Crest, where the Rich Mountain spur branches north — 7.8 km ESE of Coldwater
- **Elevation:** 1,640 m (surveyed terrain 1,640 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,229 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A steep-sided peak with a craggy summit of mica schist, oak and rhododendron on its flanks, and windswept beech on top.
- **Geographic justification:** Junction of two ridges, so it stands slightly above both; storms off Serena Bay hit it first.
- **Visual identity:** Usually the first peak to disappear into afternoon thunderheads.
- **Nearby features:** Rich Mountain, Painted Bald, Gooseberry Bald, the Parkway.
- **Why it is memorable:** Lightning-scarred snags on the summit and a reputation for the first storm of the day.

### The Steeples

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Eastern Balsam Crest, above Big Laurel Top — 3.7 km NNW of Upper Cove
- **Elevation:** 1,620 m (surveyed terrain 1,620 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,220 m within 3 km
- **Form:** rock pinnacles
- **Description:** Twin spires of steeply tilted slate rising above heath and stunted pine, joined by a narrow rocky saddle. Loose plates of rock litter the trail to the top.
- **Geographic justification:** Near-vertical beds of slate weather into knife-edges where the crest narrows.
- **Visual identity:** Two sharp points like needles on the skyline, visible from Riverside and the Tennalee River Bridge.
- **Nearby features:** Hemlock Knob, Sawyer Gap, Big Laurel spur, the Parkway.
- **Why it is memorable:** The only jagged summit on a crest of rounded domes.

### Blackstrap Knob

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** North Face, on the spur west of Mount Callahan — 5.9 km SE of Pigeonroost
- **Elevation:** 1,520 m (surveyed terrain 1,520 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,417 m within 3 km
- **Form:** knob
- **Description:** A steep knob of dark slate with a scar of fresh rock where a landslide stripped its west face to bedrock.
- **Geographic justification:** Thin soils over steeply dipping slate fail during the heavy remnant-hurricane rains that hit the North Face.
- **Visual identity:** A long pale streak down a dark green slope.
- **Nearby features:** Pigeonroost Creek, the Gap Road switchbacks, Mount Callahan.
- **Why it is memorable:** The landslide scar is visible from the Narrows Road and from boats in the strait.

### Thunderstone Mountain

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** North Face, spur between Rich Creek and Pigeonroost Creek — 5.7 km SSW of Pigeonroost
- **Elevation:** 1,450 m (surveyed terrain 1,450 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,332 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A broad, steep mountain with a summit of heath and table mountain pine and a north face of mossy boulder fields.
- **Geographic justification:** One of the parallel spurs that radiate north from the crest toward the Narrows.
- **Visual identity:** A tall, dark pyramid above Narrows Landing.
- **Nearby features:** Rich Creek, Pigeonroost Creek, the Narrows Road.
- **Why it is memorable:** Its boulder field rings hollowly under foot and holds snow weeks after the summit melts.

### Gooseberry Bald

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Western end of the Balsam Crest above Shady Gap — 6.6 km SE of Coldwater
- **Elevation:** 1,420 m (surveyed terrain 1,420 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,343 m within 3 km
- **Form:** grassy bald
- **Description:** A grassy summit with a steel fire lookout tower, a small sphagnum pond, and the first Parkway pull-off after the climb from Shady Gap.
- **Geographic justification:** The crest drops away westward here, giving an unobstructed view over the Coldwater Valley and the Hickory Tableland.
- **Visual identity:** Fire tower silhouetted on a bare summit.
- **Nearby features:** Gooseberry Pond, Sheepback Bald, Pinnacle Knob, the Parkway.
- **Why it is memorable:** Sunset from the tower sets the whole Tableland escarpment glowing orange.

### Big Laurel Top

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** North Face spur above Big Laurel village — 4.0 km SE of Big Laurel
- **Elevation:** 1,380 m (surveyed terrain 1,380 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,382 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A heavily forested summit cut by an abandoned logging railroad grade that zigzags up its western flank.
- **Geographic justification:** Spur between Big Laurel Creek and the Narrows Branch; the only North Face spur with a straight, even slope for a cable incline.
- **Visual identity:** Visible switchback scars under second-growth forest.
- **Nearby features:** Big Laurel Incline, Big Laurel Millpond, the Narrows.
- **Why it is memorable:** The old grade benches read like contour lines drawn on the mountain.

### Huckleberry Knob

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** South of Hemlock Knob, above the north arm of Calloway Lake — 5.7 km W of Upper Cove
- **Elevation:** 1,330 m (surveyed terrain 1,330 m)
- **Local relief:** 947 m within 3 km
- **Form:** knob
- **Description:** A heath-covered knob of huckleberry and mountain laurel above oak forest, with ledges looking straight down onto the lake.
- **Geographic justification:** Short spur off the crest whose thin soils support heath rather than forest.
- **Visual identity:** A rust-coloured knob in autumn above blue water.
- **Nearby features:** Calloway Lake, Upper Cove, Tennalee Cove.
- **Why it is memorable:** The best place to see the whole of Calloway Lake laid out in its bowl of mountains.

### Bearpen Knob

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Eastern end of the crest, above Hollow Reach — 3.2 km ENE of Upper Cove
- **Elevation:** 1,320 m (surveyed terrain 1,320 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,088 m within 3 km
- **Form:** knob
- **Description:** The last high point before the crest drops into Hollow Reach: a rocky knob with a sheer eastern drop and the Bearpen Tunnel beneath its southern shoulder.
- **Geographic justification:** The crest ends abruptly where the drowned gorge of Hollow Reach cuts through the massif.
- **Visual identity:** A dark headwall rising behind the western tower of the Gate Bridge.
- **Nearby features:** Sawyer Gap, the Gate Shelf, Hollow Reach, Dogwood Mountain.
- **Why it is memorable:** From the Gate Bridge it looks like the island's wall ends in a fist.

### Sheepback Bald

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** North-west spur from Gooseberry Bald — 4.3 km ESE of Coldwater
- **Elevation:** 1,280 m (surveyed terrain 1,280 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,240 m within 3 km
- **Form:** grassy bald
- **Description:** A long, smooth, grassy ridge grazed by cattle in summer, with a few wind-bent hawthorns.
- **Geographic justification:** Rounded ridge of deeply weathered schist that holds deep soil and has stayed open pasture.
- **Visual identity:** A pale green whaleback above the Coldwater Valley.
- **Nearby features:** Copperhead Knob, the Coldwater Valley, the Old Valley Road.
- **Why it is memorable:** The only high summit where cattle graze, their bells audible from the valley.

### Cove Mountain

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Spur dividing the Ledford Prong valley from Tennalee Cove — 9.8 km NNW of Halcomb Heights
- **Elevation:** 1,240 m (surveyed terrain 1,240 m)
- **Local relief:** 953 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A heavily wooded mountain with a fire road to a small cleared summit and steep coves holding old-growth tulip poplar.
- **Geographic justification:** Spur between two drainages that funnel moist air into its coves.
- **Visual identity:** Dark forested mass between the Ledford strip and the lake.
- **Nearby features:** Calloway Lake, Ledford, Little River, Calloway Dam Road.
- **Why it is memorable:** Its coves hold the largest trees on the island's south side.

### Brushy Mountain

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** South spur between Hominy Creek and Ledford Prong — 6.8 km NNW of Ledford
- **Elevation:** 1,210 m (surveyed terrain 1,210 m)
- **Local relief:** 953 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A dry, oak-and-pine mountain on the sunny side of the crest, with an old burn scar of standing dead pine.
- **Geographic justification:** South-facing slopes dry out quickly and burn in drought years.
- **Visual identity:** Lighter, patchier green than the coves around it.
- **Nearby features:** Chestnut Knob, Lake Hominy, the Gap Road.
- **Why it is memorable:** Grey snags of the old burn bristle along its crest.

### Rich Mountain

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** North-west spur off Stormhead Mountain — 6.5 km ENE of Coldwater
- **Elevation:** 1,180 m (surveyed terrain 1,180 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,182 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A rounded mountain of deep, dark soil with rich cove forests and an abandoned homestead clearing near the top.
- **Geographic justification:** Weathered mafic rock produces unusually fertile soils.
- **Visual identity:** Exceptionally lush, dark canopy; a lone stone chimney in a meadow.
- **Nearby features:** Rich Creek, Thunderstone, the Narrows Road.
- **Why it is memorable:** The chimney standing alone in its clearing.

### Sugartree Knob

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** South spur from Painted Bald above Hominy — 5.9 km NNW of Hominy
- **Elevation:** 1,150 m (surveyed terrain 1,150 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,018 m within 3 km
- **Form:** knob
- **Description:** A knob covered in sugar maple, with an old sugar-shack foundation and rusted kettles in a hollow below the summit.
- **Geographic justification:** North-facing hollows on the spur hold maples at a lower elevation than usual.
- **Visual identity:** Blazes orange in October, a week before the surrounding oaks.
- **Nearby features:** Sugartree Branch, Hominy, Painted Bald.
- **Why it is memorable:** The earliest and brightest autumn colour visible from I-21.

### Dogwood Mountain

- **Elevation:** 1,050 m (surveyed terrain 1,050 m)

- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A broad, wooded mountain whose understorey of flowering dogwood turns the slopes white in April.

### Chestnut Knob

- **Island:** Halcomb Island

- **Local relief:** 723 m within 3 km

### Copperhead Knob

- **Island:** Halcomb Island

- **Local relief:** 692 m within 3 km

### The Hogback

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Low ridge east of the Lower Tennalee Gorge, above Tennalee Falls — 3.5 km NNE of Tennalee Falls
- **Elevation:** 640 m (surveyed terrain 640 m)
- **Local relief:** 603 m within 3 km
- **Form:** ridge
- **Description:** A narrow, steep-sided ridge of hard quartzite east of the Lower Tennalee Gorge, between Calloway Dam and the falls.
- **Geographic justification:** Resistant quartzite stands up as a long hogback while the softer schist on either side has worn down.
- **Visual identity:** A sharp, wooded ridgeline above the falls town.
- **Nearby features:** Calloway Dam, Tennalee Falls, the Lower Tennalee Gorge.
- **Why it is memorable:** Its quartzite ledges catch the first sun above the fog in the gorge.

### Hickory Knob

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** Northern Hickory Tableland — 8.6 km NW of Coldwater
- **Elevation:** 585 m (surveyed terrain 585 m)
- **Local relief:** 560 m within 3 km
- **Form:** plateau knob
- **Description:** The highest point on the sandstone plateau: a low, rocky rise of conglomerate in a forest of chestnut oak and pitch pine.
- **Geographic justification:** Remnant of a thicker caprock layer on the tilted plateau surface.
- **Visual identity:** A slight swell in the otherwise level tableland horizon.
- **Nearby features:** Hickory Lake, Cane Creek gorge, the Tableland Bluffs.
- **Why it is memorable:** Pebble-studded conglomerate boulders that look like poured concrete.

### Lookout Bluff

- **Island:** Halcomb Island
- **Location:** West edge of the Hickory Tableland above Serena Bay — 3.5 km SSW of Hickory Flat
- **Elevation:** 495 m (surveyed terrain 495 m)
- **Local relief:** 483 m within 3 km
- **Form:** bluff
- **Description:** A sandstone promontory at the plateau rim, dropping 400 m to the bay in a single sweep of cliffs and wooded talus.
- **Geographic justification:** The plateau is being cut back by the sea, leaving headlands where the caprock is thickest.
- **Visual identity:** A flat-topped prow of pale rock above the water.
- **Nearby features:** Piney Cove, Hickory Tableland Airport, the Tableland Road.
- **Why it is memorable:** Sunsets over Serena Bay with the whole Mirabel shoreline in view.

## Graystone Island

### Whitlock Mountain

- **Island:** Graystone Island
- **Location:** Whitlock Range, north of Lake Whitlock — 3.2 km NE of Whitlock
- **Elevation:** 1,724 m (surveyed terrain 1,724 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,020 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** The highest summit on Graystone: a granite-cored mountain with a bare south face of exfoliating rock sheets and a summit forest of red oak, rhododendron and scattered red spruce.
- **Geographic justification:** A large granite pluton that weathers into domes and sheets rather than ridges.
- **Visual identity:** A pale, curved rock face above the village and lake.
- **Nearby features:** Whitlock, Lake Whitlock, Hawkbill Knob, Lantern Mountain.
- **Why it is memorable:** Its south face turns pink at sunrise above the still lake.

### Lantern Mountain

- **Island:** Graystone Island
- **Location:** Whitlock Range, east of Whitlock Mountain — 8.0 km ESE of Stillhouse
- **Elevation:** 1,652 m (surveyed terrain 1,652 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,584 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A broad summit topped by a white FAA radar dome and a lattice tower with red and white lights.
- **Geographic justification:** Second-highest point on the Graystone highlands with an unobstructed view out to sea.
- **Visual identity:** A white golf ball on the skyline by day, a red pulse by night.
- **Nearby features:** Bald Rock, Frostcap, Highlands Road, Ravenfork.
- **Why it is memorable:** Visible from almost every island; the radar dome is a navigation mark for small planes.

### Frostcap

- **Island:** Graystone Island
- **Location:** Eastern Whitlock Range — 8.4 km W of Fallon
- **Elevation:** 1,560 m (surveyed terrain 1,560 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,235 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A cold, north-facing summit where rime ice coats the trees through much of the winter.
- **Geographic justification:** Exposed to winter northwest winds coming off the Narrows.
- **Visual identity:** Silver-white crown of frosted trees on clear winter mornings.
- **Nearby features:** Bright Water Lake, Ravenrock, the north-shore cliffs.
- **Why it is memorable:** The only summit that looks snowy without snow.

### Sassafras Knob

- **Island:** Graystone Island
- **Location:** Western Graystone highlands — 2.3 km SW of Whitlock
- **Elevation:** 1,480 m (surveyed terrain 1,480 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,294 m within 3 km
- **Form:** knob
- **Description:** A wooded knob with a rock overlook facing south over the Blue Wall and the Piedmont strip.
- **Geographic justification:** Highland knob near the edge of the plateau escarpment.
- **Visual identity:** Dark knob behind Pale Wall Mountain's cliff.
- **Nearby features:** Pale Wall Mountain, Wolfpen Mountain, Hollow Creek.
- **Why it is memorable:** A clear view of all three granite domes in a line below.

### Pale Wall Mountain

- **Island:** Graystone Island
- **Location:** Southern edge of the highlands, west of Thunderhole Gorge — 2.9 km SSE of Whitlock
- **Elevation:** 1,440 m (surveyed terrain 1,440 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,207 m within 3 km
- **Form:** cliff mountain
- **Description:** A mountain whose south face is a sheer 230 m wall of white granite streaked with black lichen and seep stains.
- **Geographic justification:** Exfoliation joints parallel to the escarpment peel off huge sheets of granite.
- **Visual identity:** A white wall visible from the sound and from Calder.
- **Nearby features:** Glassface Dome, Thunderhole Gorge, the Whitlock Grade.
- **Why it is memorable:** The largest single sheet of bare rock in the islands.

### Ravenrock

- **Island:** Graystone Island
- **Location:** Eastern highlands above Fallon — 5.3 km WSW of Fallon
- **Elevation:** 1,390 m (surveyed terrain 1,390 m)
- **Local relief:** 1,191 m within 3 km
- **Form:** mountain
- **Description:** A summit with a band of dark gneiss cliffs where ravens and peregrine falcons nest.
- **Geographic justification:** Dark gneiss roof pendant over the granite, more resistant than the rock around it.
- **Visual identity:** Dark rock band under a wooded summit.
- **Nearby features:** Hornet Spire, Fallon Knob, Highlands Road.
