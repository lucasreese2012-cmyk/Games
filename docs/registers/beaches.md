## Mirabel Island

### Mirabel Beach

- **Island:** Mirabel Island
- **Location:** 1.0 km W of Mirabel Beach (grid 3.9 E / 44.6 N)
- **Length:** 3.2 km
- **Sand:** quartz
- **Description:** The main resort beach: a wide, gently sloping strand of sugar-white quartz sand in front of condominium towers, with lifeguard stands and rows of umbrellas.
- **Geographic justification:** Quartz sand carried down from the crystalline highlands by the rivers and moved south along the coast by waves; the low-energy Gulf side keeps it fine and clean.
- **Visual identity:** Squeaky white sand, emerald-green nearshore water turning deep blue past the sandbar.
- **Nearby features:** Mirabel Pier, Pier District, Lake Mirabel, Beach Road.
- **Why it is memorable:** Sunset straight out over Serena Bay behind the pier.
